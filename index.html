TRIFFID Visual Perception Engine

TRIFFID develops an integrated approach for maximizing the efficiency of First Responders’ field operations through remote and smart robotic reconnaissance in disaster sites. The project integrates AI-powered UGV/UAV robotic platforms, augmented-reality ground-station interfaces, and real-time situational awareness tools into European Civil Protection procedures.

This project has received funding from the European Union’s Horizon Europe research and innovation programme under grant agreement No.101168042.
